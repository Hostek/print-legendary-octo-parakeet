# Configuring XeLaTeX on Overleaf

Overleaf is a cloud-based LaTeX editor. By default, new projects are compiled using pdfLaTeX. If you want to use modern system fonts or specialized Unicode setups, you can switch the compiler to XeLaTeX.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Log in to your account at [Overleaf.com](https://www.overleaf.com) and open your project.
2. In the top-left corner of the screen, click the **File** button (located above the file list panel).
3. Under the **Settings** section, locate the **Compiler** option.
4. Click the dropdown menu (which likely says *pdfLaTeX* by default) and select **XeLaTeX**.
5. Close the menu panel by clicking the **Menu** button again or clicking outside of it.
6. Click the green **Recompile** button (or press `Ctrl + Enter` / `Cmd + Enter`) to rebuild your document with the new compiler settings.
